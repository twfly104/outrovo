Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what Outrovo collects, why, and the control you have over it.

Who is responsible

The data controller for your account data is Outrovo Inc. For privacy questions or to exercise any right below, email privacy@outrovo.com — we answer within 30 days.

What we collect

  • •Account data — your name, work email and company when you sign up, plus your consent record (which terms you accepted and when).
  • •Workspace data — the prospects, campaigns and sequences you create. This is your data, stored only to run the product.
  • •Usage data — basic product analytics (pages viewed, features used) so we can improve Outrovo.
  • •Billing data — processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store full card numbers.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • •Contract — account and workspace data, to provide the service you signed up for.
  • •Consent — your acceptance of the terms and this policy at signup, recorded with a timestamp.
  • •Legitimate interest — usage analytics and security logging, balanced against your rights.

Your prospects' data

When you upload prospect lists, you are the data controller and Outrovo acts as your processor, handling that data only on your instructions. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact your prospects and for honoring their opt-outs — the product enforces one-click unsubscribe and a suppression list automatically. A data processing addendum (DPA) is available on request at privacy@outrovo.com.

What we never do

  • ✓We never sell your data or your prospect lists.
  • ✓We never read the content of your replies for advertising.
  • ✓We never email your prospects except as part of campaigns you start.

How we protect it

Passwords are salted and hashed (scrypt) — never stored in plain text. Connected inbox passwords are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Sessions use httpOnly cookies. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to production data is limited to the engineers who operate the service.

How long we keep it

  • •Account & workspace data — kept while your account is active; erased immediately when you delete your account.
  • •Sessions — expire after 7 days.
  • •Activity logs — rolling 500-event operational log, kept for security and debugging.
  • •Suppression records — kept after deletion only where a customer asks us to keep honoring an opt-out.

Who processes it

  • •Stripe — payment processing.
  • •Hosting provider — infrastructure (Render/Vercel).
  • •Email delivery — Resend or your own connected inbox (SMTP), only to send campaigns you start.

Where these providers process data outside the EEA, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Your rights

You can exercise every one of these from the app, today, without contacting us:

  • •Access & portability — Settings → Account → Export my data downloads everything your account holds as JSON.
  • •Rectification — edit your prospects, campaigns and profile in the app.
  • •Erasure — Settings → Account → Delete account removes the account and all its data immediately.
  • •Objection & restriction — email privacy@outrovo.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Cookies

We use one strictly-necessary cookie (outrovo_session, httpOnly, 7 days) to keep you signed in. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@outrovo.com and we will get back to you.
