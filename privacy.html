Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains what Outrovo collects, why, and the control you have over it.

What we collect

  • •Account data — your name, work email and company when you sign up.
  • •Workspace data — the prospects, campaigns and sequences you create. This is your data, stored only to run the product.
  • •Usage data — basic product analytics (pages viewed, features used) so we can improve Outrovo.
  • •Billing data — processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store full card numbers.

What we never do

  • ✓We never sell your data or your prospect lists.
  • ✓We never read the content of your replies for advertising.
  • ✓We never email your prospects except as part of campaigns you start.

How we protect it

Passwords are salted and hashed (scrypt) — never stored in plain text. Sessions use httpOnly cookies. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to production data is limited to the engineers who operate the service.

Your controls

You can export or delete your prospects at any time from the app. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the demo booking page and we will get back to you.

This is a template policy provided for launch readiness — have it reviewed by your legal advisor before operating at scale.
