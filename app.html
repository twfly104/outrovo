          Settings

          Sender accounts

          Connect the inboxes you send from. Campaigns rotate across them automatically, and each inbox has its own daily cap. Turn on warmup ramp-up for new accounts — the cap starts small and grows each day so mailbox providers build trust in the address.

          Domain health

          Onboarding diagnostic for your signup domain — run it before your first campaign to catch SPF/DKIM/DMARC problems that would tank deliverability.

          LinkedIn safety

          LinkedIn actions are paced to stay inside human-normal limits. The daily budget caps how many tasks the queue surfaces per day; new tasks spread across a 6-hour window instead of appearing all at once.

          LinkedIn autopilot bridge

          Use an external autopilot (PhantomBuster, Zapier, your own scripts) to execute the LinkedIn tasks Outrovo queues. Generate a token, then have the tool POST outcomes back — Outrovo marks tasks done and stops the email sequence when a prospect replies on LinkedIn.

          Suppression list

          Anyone who unsubscribes lands here and is skipped by every campaign — including future imports. Every email you send carries a one-click unsubscribe link automatically.

          Sending engine

          Sending uses RESEND_API_KEY (HTTP, recommended) when set, falling back to SMTP (SMTP_HOST/SMTP_PORT/SMTP_USER/SMTP_PASS/SMTP_FROM). With neither, sends are logged as demos.
