        LinkedIn automationdaily safety budget · autopilot bridge

        Daily safety budget

        LinkedIn actions are paced to stay inside human-normal limits. The budget caps how many tasks the queue surfaces per day; new tasks spread across a 6-hour window instead of appearing all at once.

        Autopilot bridge

        Point an external autopilot (PhantomBuster, Zapier, your own scripts) at the callback URL — Outrovo marks tasks done and stops the email sequence when a prospect replies on LinkedIn.

        Suppression listunsubscribes blocked everywhere, automatically

        Anyone who unsubscribes lands here and is skipped by every campaign — including future imports. Every email you send carries a one-click unsubscribe link automatically.

        CRM & automationpush sends, replies and clicks into your stack

        Point a Zapier catch hook, a HubSpot/Pipedrive workflow webhook, or any HTTPS endpoint at Outrovo — events fan out as JSON, optionally signed with your secret.

        Sending enginehow mail actually leaves Outrovo

        Sending uses RESEND_API_KEY (HTTP, recommended) when set, falling back to SMTP (SMTP_HOST/SMTP_PORT/SMTP_USER/SMTP_PASS/SMTP_FROM). With neither, sends are logged as demos. Inboxes you connect above always take priority over the server gateway.

        ComplianceCAN-SPAM mailing address

        CAN-SPAM requires your valid physical postal address in every campaign email. Whatever you save here is added to the footer of every email you send, above the unsubscribe link.

        Accountlog out · export · erase (GDPR)

        GDPR data rights: download everything your account holds, or erase the account and all its campaigns, prospects, replies, tasks and inboxes. Erasure is immediate and cannot be undone.
