    Sender accounts

    Paste your email and an Google app password — takes 30 seconds.

    Campaigns rotate across every connected inbox, each with its own daily cap. Warmup ramp-up starts new inboxes small and grows the cap each day, so mailbox providers build trust in the address.

    Optional: sync replies into Outrovoneeds a one-time domain setup by your admin

    Replies to your campaigns already arrive in your own Gmail/Outlook inbox as usual — nothing breaks without this. Forwarding is only needed if you also want them synced into Outrovo's Inbox (auto-stops the sequence, AI drafts, intent labels).

    ⚠️ This address only becomes valid once your admin points the inbound domain's DNS mail records at Outrovo (INBOUND_DOMAIN + MX). Gmail will show "invalid forwarding address" until that one-time setup is done.
